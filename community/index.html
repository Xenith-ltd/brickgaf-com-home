Community

The goal is to make collecting accessible and fun while building a friendly community of LEGO® fans.

Come to a show

Follow brickgaf on Whatnot to hear when a show starts, then come along, watch the bidding and join in the chat.

Follow Brick Gaf on Whatnot

House rules

For the show chat and every message:

  • Be kind: everyone was new once.
  • Keep it on Whatnot: every sale, offer and payment goes through Whatnot.
  • No spam and no selling of your own.
  • Keep personal details out of the chat.
  • Have fun with it.

LEGO® fans in Ireland

Brick.ie, a LEGO® fan group in Ireland, runs Brick Féile, a fan show near Dublin that raises money for the charity Fairy Bricks.
