Privacy

Darren Kane, trading as Brick Gaf, is the controller of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What we collect: the name, email address, topic and message you send through the contact form or by email. Nothing else: this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Why: to read and answer your message and, for suppliers and people offering stock, to discuss doing business. The lawful basis is legitimate interests: replying to people who get in touch.

Who handles it: FormSubmit delivers the contact form to our mailbox by email, Fastmail hosts that mailbox, and GitHub hosts this website and keeps its own server logs, including visitors' IP addresses, for security. Some of these services are based outside the UK.

How long: messages are kept as long as needed to deal with them and deleted within 12 months, unless we go on to work together.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or object to how they are used, by writing to hello@brickgaf.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
