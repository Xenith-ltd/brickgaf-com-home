Buying on Whatnot

  1. Set up your account

    Add a payment method and a shipping address in the Whatnot app or on whatnot.com. Whatnot needs both before you can bid or buy.

  2. Follow brickgaf

    Follow Brick Gaf on Whatnot and you get a notification when a show goes live.

  3. Join the show

    Watch live in the Whatnot app or on whatnot.com.

  4. Bid or buy

    Tap to bid, or set a maximum bid. Timers vary: on some items a late bid adds a few seconds; on Sudden Death items it adds none. Some items are Buy It Now. Bids are binding.

  5. Pay and track

    Whatnot takes payment and adds shipping and any taxes at checkout. Whatnot Buyer Protection covers eligible orders that don't arrive, arrive damaged or aren't as described; time limits and exclusions apply.

Whatnot accounts are for over-18s. Availability, fees and shipping depend on where you and the seller are. Whatnot's rules apply and can change (last checked October 2026): How to buy · Bidding · Buyer Protection · Whatnot help centre
